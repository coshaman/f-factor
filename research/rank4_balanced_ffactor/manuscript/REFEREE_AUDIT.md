# Adversarial referee audit

The manuscript was reviewed as an independent submission, with the following
potential rejection points tested.

* The 2017 source explicitly states the old rank-five construction and calls
  rank four the only open balanced case; no equivalent rank-four result was
  found in the final cross-terminology search.
* A cross-gadget strong odd cycle cannot arise: non-size-two edges have
  private vertices and source ports are size two; the center case is trapped
  in a 2-cycle, while the center-avoiding private pattern is a path.
* The local factor equations have no unlisted mode because every indicator
  is binary and \(b_1=b_2\) is forced.
* Perfect \(f\)-matching multiplicities are bounded by a vertex with
  \(f=1\) on every edge.
* Bounded-occurrence 3DM-3 supplies the exact source restriction needed for
  the degree bound.
* Expansion replaces one endpoint by one clone and therefore does not raise
  rank; the two-stage expansion identity is valid for arbitrary expansion
  vectors.
* Mengerian and ideal are not conflated: Mengerianity is proved first, and
  idealness is used only as a corollary.
* The degree-15 calculation counts five copies of each source port and no
  other source incidences.
* The paper does not claim that the concepts of expansion or Mengerian
  hypergraphs are new, and it excludes uniform rank-four and counting claims.

Conclusion: no fatal issue was found. The result is presented as a concise
theoretical manuscript, with computational checks explicitly labeled
supplementary rather than evidentiary proof.
