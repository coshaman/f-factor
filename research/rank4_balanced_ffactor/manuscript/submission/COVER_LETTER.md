# Cover letter

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Closing the Rank-Four Gap for f-Factors in
Balanced Hypergraphs” for publication in the *European Journal of
Combinatorics*.

The manuscript resolves the previously open rank-four case for non-uniform
balanced hypergraph f-factors. We give a rank-four balanced selector gadget
and prove NP-completeness for both f-factors and perfect f-matchings, with
maximum degree at most 10 and maximum demand at most 5. A rank-preserving
vertex expansion then yields NP-completeness of ordinary Perfect Matching
for Mengerian, and hence ideal, hypergraphs of rank at most four and maximum
degree at most 15.

The contribution is intended as a concise structural-complexity result. The
main proof is self-contained apart from clearly cited prior rank-three
algorithmic and rank-five hardness results. Exact computational checks are
provided only as supplementary falsification evidence. The manuscript does
not claim uniform rank-four hardness, a max-demand-four bound, or a counting
complexity result.

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. The author and
affiliation fields in the repository copy are placeholders pending insertion
of the submitting authors' metadata.

Thank you for your consideration.
