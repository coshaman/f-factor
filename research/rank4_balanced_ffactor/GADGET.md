# Candidate gadget

For a source triple (e=(v_1,v_2,v_3)), introduce private vertices
(c_e,h_{e,1},\ldots,h_{e,4},r_{e,1},r_{e,2}). Add

\[
U_i=\{c_e,h_i\}\ (1\le i\le4),
\quad B_1=\{c_e,h_1,h_2,r_1\},
\quad B_2=\{c_e,h_3,h_4,r_2\},
\]

\[
A=\{c_e,r_1,r_2\},
\quad P_j=\{c_e,v_j\}\ (1\le j\le3).
\]

Set (f(c_e)=5) and (f(w)=1) for every other vertex. Subscripts (e)
are suppressed in local calculations.
