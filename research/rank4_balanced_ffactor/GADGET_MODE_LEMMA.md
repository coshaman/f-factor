# Gadget mode lemma

Let the indicators of (U_i,B_1,B_2,A,P_j) in an (f)-factor be
(u_i,b_1,b_2,a,p_j\in\{0,1\}). The private vertices (r_1,r_2) give

\[
b_1+a=1,qquad b_2+a=1.
\]

Thus (b_1=b_2=b) and (a=1-b). The four (h_i)-equations give

\[
u_i+b=1,
\]

so (u_i=1-b). Finally the center equation is

\[
4(1-b)+2b+(1-b)+\sum_{j=1}^3p_j=5,
\]

and therefore (sum_jp_j=3b).

If (b=0), all (u_i=1), (a=1), and all (p_j=0). If (b=1), both
(B)-edges are selected, all (u_i) and (A) are unselected, and all
(P_j) are selected. These are the only cases because (b) is binary.

Conversely, each listed case satisfies every local private-vertex and center
equation. Hence every factor induces exactly one of the two modes.
