# Paper outline

1. Define balanced hypergraphs, f-factors, and perfect f-matchings.
2. Review the rank-at-most-three algorithm and rank-at-least-five hardness
   boundary of Beckenbach–Scheidweiler.
3. Present the rank-four gadget and prove its two modes algebraically.
4. Prove global balancedness by isolating cycles containing a center and
   cycles avoiding all centers.
5. Reduce 3DM and establish NP membership.
6. Observe that every target edge contains an (f=1) vertex, converting
   perfect f-matchings to f-factors.
7. State the resulting non-uniform rank dichotomy and include the exact
   independent audit as supplementary verification.
