Proved consequences of the frozen rank-four balanced construction:

* balanced f-factor and perfect f-matching remain NP-complete with rank at
  most four, maximum degree at most ten, and max f at most five;
* vertex expansion preserves rank, and transforms perfect f-matching into
  ordinary perfect matching;
* the expanded instances are Mengerian, with rank at most four and maximum
  degree at most fifteen;
* consequently ordinary Perfect Matching is NP-complete for Mengerian, and
  hence ideal, hypergraphs under those bounds.
