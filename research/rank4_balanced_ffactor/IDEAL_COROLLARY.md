# Ideal-hypergraph corollary

Every Mengerian hypergraph is ideal: the Mengerian min-max property implies
integrality of the associated vertex-cover polyhedron. Therefore the same
instances in `MENGERIAN_RANK4_HARDNESS.md` are ideal. Consequently Perfect
Matching is NP-complete on ideal hypergraphs of rank at most four, with the
same optional maximum-degree bound 15. This is a weaker class restriction
than the Mengerian theorem, not a stronger one.
